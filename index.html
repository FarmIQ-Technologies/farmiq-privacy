Privacy Policy

1. Who can use FarmIQ

FarmIQ is intended for farm operators, workers, agencies, suppliers, training sites, and service providers (e.g. technicians and veterinarians) who are 18 years of age or older and able to enter a binding agreement. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us data, contact our Data Protection Officer and we will delete it.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run the service.

a. Information you give us

  • Identity: first name, last name, middle name, and profile photo.
  • Contact: mobile phone number.
  • Security credential: your PIN, stored only as a one-way hash — we never see or store your plain PIN.
  • Address & location: region, province, city/municipality, barangay, and street address (Philippine Standard Geographic Code). Optionally, a precise GPS pin you choose to drop when booking a technician visit or requesting a product delivery.
  • Farm and livestock records you enter: farm profile, animals, health and treatment events, milk, weight, breeding/farrowing/kidding, mortality, biosecurity checks, sales, expenses, and related notes.
  • Business/provider details (partners only): business name, contact person, contact number, service categories, livestock types served, and professional license number (e.g. PRC license for veterinarians).
  • Transaction records: orders, sales, and payment entries. Payment method is free text (e.g. "Cash", "GCash"). We do not collect or store card numbers, bank credentials, or process card payments.
  • Uploaded files: photos and documents you attach (e.g. job-application documents, product images, order receipts, visit reports, training application files).

b. Information we collect automatically

  • Device & push: push-notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and platform (iOS/Android). This token is not used for advertising.
  • Technical & security logs: IP address, device identifier, app/user-agent information, and activity audit logs used for security, fraud prevention, and troubleshooting.
  • One-time passwords: your phone number and a hashed OTP during phone verification.

We do not collect: browsing history, contacts, call logs, or any data unrelated to the agricultural services FarmIQ provides. We do not use your data for targeted advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

3. Device permissions

  • Camera & photos: to capture or upload profile pictures, product photos, and documents attached to applications or orders. The camera is never accessed in the background or outside an action you start. You may deny it and upload from your gallery instead.
  • Location (precise): only when you choose to drop a map pin for a technician booking or a delivery. You may decline; the app still works using your saved address.
  • Notifications: to deliver booking, order, job, and application updates via push.

5. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with service providers ("personal information processors") that help us run the app, under contract and only as needed:

  • Google Firebase / Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notifications (receives your push token). Google Privacy Policy
  • Cloudinary — stores and serves your uploaded images and documents. Cloudinary Privacy Policy
  • Iprog (SMS gateway) — sends OTP and booking SMS (receives your phone number and the message content).
  • Expo (EAS) — app distribution and updates. Expo Privacy Policy

We also share information between users as needed to deliver a service you initiate — for example, when you book a technician, that technician sees your name, farm name, contact details, location, and concern. We do not sell your personal data. We may disclose data when required by Philippine law, a court order, a government authority, or to prevent harm.

6. Data storage and security

  • PINs are stored as one-way hashes; we never store the plain PIN.
  • Sessions use short-lived access tokens and HttpOnly refresh tokens.
  • All data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel and processes.
  • We keep audit logs of sensitive actions.

No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach affects your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and the National Privacy Commission (NPC) as required by law, within 72 hours of discovery.

7. Data retention

We keep your personal data for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service. OTPs are short-lived and expire quickly; security/audit logs are kept for a limited period for fraud prevention and legal compliance. When you delete your account (see below), we deactivate it immediately and delete or irreversibly anonymize your personal data within 90 days, except records we are legally required to retain. Some backups are purged on a rolling schedule.

8. Your rights (RA 10173)

As a data subject under Philippine law, you have the right to: be informed; access your data; correct inaccurate or incomplete data; object to processing; have data erased or blocked; data portability; and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission at www.privacy.gov.ph. To exercise any right, contact our DPO (below) or use the in-app controls. We may verify your identity before acting and will respond within the period required by the NPC.

9. Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and personal data:

  • In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • By email: email farmiqtechnologies@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and the phone number associated with your account.
  • On the web: this page — https://farmiq-technologies.github.io/farmiq-privacy/ (reachable without logging in).

On deletion your account is deactivated immediately and can no longer sign in. We delete or anonymize your personal data and farm records within 90 days, except data we are legally required to retain.
Data deleted: account info, profile, uploaded files, push token, and personal records.
Data retained: transaction records required by law (if any), retained in anonymized form.

10. Children

FarmIQ is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor has registered, contact our DPO at farmiqtechnologies@gmail.com and we will deactivate the account and delete the data promptly.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy. When we make a material change, we update the version and effective date and ask you to review and accept the new version in the app before continuing to use the service. Continued use after acceptance constitutes agreement to the updated policy.
