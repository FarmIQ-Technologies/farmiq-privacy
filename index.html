FarmIQ Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 7, 2026
Last updated: May 7, 2026

FarmIQ ("we," "our," or "us") operates the FarmIQ mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our app. By using FarmIQ, you agree to the terms of this policy.

This policy complies with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations.

1. Who Can Use FarmIQ

FarmIQ is an agricultural platform designed for farmers, farm workers, agencies, and suppliers. The app is intended for users 13 years of age and older.

Users between the ages of 13 and 17 may use FarmIQ only with the knowledge and consent of a parent or legal guardian. By using the app, users under 18 confirm that their parent or guardian is aware of and consents to their use of the platform.

We recognize that in agricultural communities, younger family members (aged 13 and above) may assist parents or guardians in operating the app. This is permitted provided a parent or legal guardian has given consent and takes responsibility for the minor's account activity.

We do not knowingly allow children under 13 to create accounts or use FarmIQ independently. If we discover that a user is under 13 without verifiable parental consent, we will deactivate the account and delete associated data promptly. If you believe a child under 13 has registered, contact us immediately at farmiqtechnologies@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Account information: Full name, phone number, role (Farmer, Worker, Agency, Supplier), and address provided during registration.
  • Profile and content data: Photos, profile pictures, product images, and documents (such as job application files) that you upload.
  • Transaction data: Orders placed, job listings created, job applications submitted, and related activity.
  • Device and usage data: Device type, operating system, app version, and general usage patterns for troubleshooting and improvement purposes.
  • Push notification token: A device token used to deliver push notifications relevant to your account (e.g., order updates, job application status). This token is not used for advertising.

We do not collect: precise GPS location, browsing history, contacts, call logs, or any data unrelated to the agricultural services provided by FarmIQ.

3. Camera Permission

FarmIQ requests access to your device camera for the following specific purposes only:

  • Capturing product photos for supplier listings
  • Uploading profile pictures
  • Attaching photos to job applications or orders

The camera is never accessed in the background, during calls, or for any purpose outside of what you explicitly initiate within the app. We do not record video. You may deny camera permission and instead upload photos from your device gallery.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To connect farmers, workers, agencies, and suppliers on the platform
  • To process and fulfill orders and job applications
  • To send push notifications about your account activity (e.g., new job posted, order status, application updates)
  • To improve app performance, fix bugs, and develop new features
  • To comply with legal obligations under Philippine law

We do not use your data for targeted advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

5. Legal Basis for Processing (Philippine Data Privacy Act)

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act, we process personal data based on:

  • Consent: You consent to data collection when you register and use the app.
  • Contractual necessity: Processing is required to provide the services you requested (e.g., displaying your products, processing orders).
  • Legitimate interests: Improving app stability and security.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Between platform users: Profile information (name, role, general location) is visible to other FarmIQ users as part of the platform's core function (e.g., agencies viewing farmer applications, buyers viewing supplier listings).
  • Service providers: We use third-party services to operate FarmIQ, including cloud storage (Cloudinary for files and images) and push notification services (Firebase Cloud Messaging). These providers access only the data necessary to perform their function and are bound by data protection agreements.
  • Legal requirements: We will disclose data if required by Philippine law, court order, or government authority.
  • Safety: If we believe disclosure is necessary to prevent harm to a user or the public.

7. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers. We implement the following security measures:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit
  • Access controls limiting who within our team can access personal data
  • Regular security reviews

No system is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach that affects your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and the National Privacy Commission (NPC) as required by law within 72 hours of discovery.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

9. Your Rights (Philippine Data Privacy Act)

As a data subject under Philippine law, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Object: Object to processing of your data in certain circumstances
  • Portability: Request your data in a structured, commonly used format
  • Lodge a complaint: File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC) at www.privacy.gov.ph

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at farmiqtechnologies@gmail.com. We will respond within 15 business days.

10. Parental Rights

Parents or legal guardians of minor users (ages 13–17) may:

  • Request to review the personal data collected about their child
  • Request correction or deletion of their child's data
  • Withdraw consent and request account deactivation at any time

Contact us at farmiqtechnologies@gmail.com with the subject line "Minor Account Request."

11. Third-Party Services

FarmIQ uses the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google): Push notifications — Google Privacy Policy
  • Cloudinary: File and image storage — Cloudinary Privacy Policy
  • Expo (EAS): App distribution and updates — Expo Privacy Policy

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will notify users via the app or email. Continued use of FarmIQ after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Account Deletion

To request deletion of your account and associated data:

  1. Email farmiqtechnologies@gmail.com with subject line "Account Deletion Request"
  2. Include the phone number associated with your account
  3. We will delete your account and associated data within 30 days

Data deleted: account info, profile, orders, job applications, and uploaded files.
Data retained: transaction records required by law (if any).
